
**ch 1**
- Arjuna is sad

**ch 2**
- The body is perishable but the soul is not, death is not something to grieve
- One’s only duty at any time is selfless action
- The Man of Perfect Wisdom is free from all desires, like the ocean

**ch3**
- The paths of knowledge, action, and others are all really the same
- Vasanas are impressions of past actions on the mind, you must control them
- Meditation is to purify the intellect, and the intellect is used to control the mind, preventing desires

**ch4**
- Constant and diligent self-discipline
